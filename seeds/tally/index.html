Room tally card

Your AI, as files you own. Learn it in an hour. Run it on any computer. Hand it to anyone. One card per room. Numbers only, never names.

Date and place
Your host number (the person who asked you to host gives it to you)
1. How many arrived (someone who comes twice counts twice)
2. How many had Copilot ready when they arrived
3. Finished: at the start everyone told their Brainstem one fact. How many finished all six steps within 60 minutes, then closed it, opened it again and got the fact back?
4. Of those who finished, how many had never written code
5. How many said they want to host a room
6. You: did you first come to a room as a learner? (yes or no)
7. You: did someone ask you to host, or did you find it yourself?
The hardest step today (one or two words)
8. A week later, ask once in the room's group chat: "If you finished, are you still using it?" How many said yes?

Text the numbers to the person who asked you to host. That is all we count. Run the room with the room guide.
